What changed, and what it costs

Three colours, three jobs. Midnight navy is structure and trust (band, ink, type label). Marigold is desire: only the Buy button and the % badge. Green is reward: only the saving. Nothing else gets colour, so each one lands.
Photos carry the page. 44 % of the card, on a lit stone tile with the photo's white background blended away. The detail hero is 5:4. Today's originals are 200 px, so Q-020 (store larger images) becomes a must.
Type with a voice. Jost at 26 / 38 / 44 for prices, Plus Jakarta Sans for everything else. Inter is gone.
Motion, cheap. Cards rise in with a 60 ms stagger, badges pop, the live dot breathes, buttons squash on press. CSS transform and opacity only. Sticky bar is the one glass surface.
