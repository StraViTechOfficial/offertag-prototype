2 · Button

Four variants, three sizes

Rule: one Primary per screen. Secondary for the quieter companion, Ghost for cancel and "show more" links that act like buttons, Destructive for admin deletes only and always behind a confirm. Pressed state required, hover optional (in-app browsers). Gold never. Loading keeps the width and hides the label.

4 · Chip

Select, Applied, Trigger

8 · Overlays

Sheet on phone, dialog or drawer on desktop

Rule: one scrim, one open at a time. Close by ✕, Esc, back button or tapping the scrim. Focus is trapped inside and returned on close; page scroll locks. Sheets have a handle and a fixed header and footer. 200 ms in, 150 ms out, instant under reduced motion. A second step replaces the body, never a second overlay.

Try them

Esc closes. Sheets are phone-shaped: on desktop they still rise from the bottom here so you can judge the motion; the real desktop uses the dialog or drawer beside them.

9 · Toast, loader, skeleton, banner, empty state

Feedback

Rule: one toast at a time, bottom, above any sticky bar; 4 s, or 10 s when it carries Undo; pauses while focused. Never for form errors. Admin toasts name the count and the thing. Loader is a 24 px ring under the last card. Skeleton is static blocks, no shimmer. Banners use text plus icon, never colour alone.
