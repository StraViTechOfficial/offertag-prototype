# Experience brief — deal card behaviour and deal detail page
AGREED v1 · 2026-11-09 (D-049, D-050) · v2 additions AGREED (D-058) after the parity pass, marked [P] · Law 12. Trust line removed per D-049.

## Who arrives, and from where
1. From the feed (tap on a card): already saw price and store; wants the missing pieces fast (what exactly, any conditions, is it still worth it) and the buy button.
2. From a chat link (`/d/{id}` or `/deal/{slug}` shared on WhatsApp/Telegram): cold arrival in an in-app browser; has not seen the feed; needs the whole picture on one screen and a reason to trust the site. The biggest audience for this page.
3. From Google (search "X price drop"): same as 2, plus wants to know the site is legitimate and what else is here.
4. Admin verifying a posted deal: wants to see exactly what users see.

## Jobs
- Decide in one glance: product, price, MRP, saving, store, how fresh.
- Understand conditions: coupon or bank offer steps (`deal_step`), caption, description, minimum order.
- Act: buy at the store; share to a group; copy link.
- Continue: related deals (same category / same store), back to the list without losing position.
- Trust: who curated it, when, where the link goes, honest state if withdrawn.

## Behaviours
Card tap (choice 3)
- Card body tap → detail. The card's "Buy at Store" → `/d/{id}` click-out directly, no detail in between. Share icon → share sheet. Three distinct targets, each with its own hit area ≥ 44 px.
- Desktop add-on to decide: card tap opens a slide-over detail beside the grid (URL becomes `/deal/{slug}`), full page via "Open full page" or direct link. Phone always full page.

Detail content, in reading order
1. Image (white well, large; zoom on tap for phone), discount badge.
2. Type label · store (logo + name) · posted time.
3. Title.
4. Price block: current price (Jost, large), labelled MRP struck, "Save ₹x (67%)".
5. Primary action "Buy at {Store}" — phone: sticky bottom bar with price + button; desktop: in the sticky right panel. Below it one line: "Opens {store} in a new tab. We may earn a commission." (plain disclosure, research).
6. Share row: WhatsApp, Telegram, copy link.
7. How to get it: `deal_step` as numbered steps; caption; description.
8. Related deals: same category then same store, feed component, 6–8 cards, hybrid paging off (fixed set + "See all in {category}").
9. Report a problem: "Link not working / price changed" (feeds admin; existing feedback table). Needs confirmation it is wanted.

States
- Withdrawn/rejected deal: page stays (SEO), header banner "This deal is no longer available", price block greyed, buy button replaced by "See similar deals", related feed prominent. HTTP 410 or 200 with noindex is a dev decision.
- Missing image: white well with type icon. Missing MRP: show price only, no fake saving.
- Arrival from a chat link in an in-app browser: no cookie/consent banners, no app-install interstitial; header shows logo and a visible "More deals" path to the feed.

SEO and sharing
- Server-rendered with Product/Offer structured data, canonical `/deal/{slug}`; `/v/{slug}` and `/d/{id}` keep working as today.
- OG image = the deal image on a branded white card (generated by the frontend at request time) so WhatsApp previews show price and store.

## Feel
Confident and quick: the price block answers the question; the rest supports it. Calm chrome, one accent (badge, saving), no competing calls to action.

## Never
No second "Buy" button with a different label. No countdown. No "X people viewing". No login wall. No auto-redirect to the store. No modal on arrival.
