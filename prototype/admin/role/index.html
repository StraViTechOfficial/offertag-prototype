Roles

What each role can open in the admin. Roles are fixed; to give someone a different role, ask the developer.

Admin

Everything, including settings and users.

Sub-admin

Posts and manages deals. Sees Incoming, Deals and View site only.

Member

Signs in on the website or app to comment and get alerts. No admin access. Listed under Members.
