Why BLDC ceiling fans cost more, and when they pay off

OfferTag · 30 Oct · 1 min read

BLDC is a type of motor. Fans built with one use much less electricity than regular ceiling fans, but they cost more to buy. Whether that is worth it depends on how many hours a day the fan runs.

Who should switch

If a fan runs most of the day, the lower power use adds up. For a room used now and then, a regular fan is often the better buy.
