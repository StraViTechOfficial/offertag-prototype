What each role can open in the admin. Roles are fixed; to give someone a different role, ask the developer.

Admin

Everything, including settings and users.

Sub-admin

Posts and manages deals. Sees Deals and View site only.

Member

Signs in on the website or app to save deals and comment. No admin access.
