About Clothing

Mens' Clothing
Mens clothing mainly includes t-shirts, shirts, jeans, shorts, active and daily wear clothes, innerwears, winter wears like jackets and trousers. Best selling brands in India are US Polo Association, Lee, Levis, UCB (United Colors Of Benetton), Arrow, Raymond, Wrangler, Pepe (jeans), Puma, Myanmar and Jockey (innerwears).

Women clothing
Girls and Women in India are very fond of shopping clothes. They would buy clothes on every big and small festival. Wearing different dresses on each occasion is necessary for women in India. Women's clothing includes ethnic wear, western wear, lingerie and nightwears including innerwears and sportwear.
Western Wear is very trending for girls and ladies of young ages and includes midi skirts, skirts, dresses (including one-piece and partywear) dresses, jeans and leggings, trousers and capris, tops, tees and what not. Ethnic wear is customary for all festivals in India and includes saree, salwar, kurtis and kurtas. Special tees, track pants, tights and leggings are also very famous for sportswear for girls. Shorts, trousers, half-pants, hot pants are also very popular among Indian girls. Major brands for women clothing includes Biba, Vero moda, Miss chase, Marks & Spencer, W for Women, Harpa, Aurelia, Rangriti, Amadore, Lee, Levis' and hundreds of other brands.

Kids' Clothing
Poeple in Indian care about their kids more than themselves. Several Kids clothing are available online like school dresses and shoes, kids shirts, pants, half-pants and trousers. You can buy almost everything you could buy for Men's and women's clothing for Kids as well.
