OfferTag · palette themes

All three derive from the logo navy #082454. Same navy scale, same navy-tinted neutrals, same type. Only the accent role changes. Each is shown in light and dark with the same sample content. Research rules applied: Fresh is the default tab; the image well stays white in dark mode because product photos are white; price hierarchy is current → MRP → "Save ₹x" in the accent; "Buy at Store" names the destination; WhatsApp share is a card action; expired is text + icon + greyscale with the CTA disabled; red is reserved for form errors; every badge passes 4.5:1 in both modes. Scoring in research-to-brand.md.

A · Ink — navy-led monochrome

  • No accent colour. Emphasis comes from weight, size and navy tints.
  • Discount badge is a navy tint. Price is plain strong text.
  • Research: best for calm and CVD safety; weakest card scan-ability at 360 px for older users (research-to-brand #4, #8, #10).

B · Royal — navy + antique gold

  • One warm accent, used only for the discount badge, shop label and a 2px nav rule.
  • Gold is muted (#C49A3C) so it reads premium rather than yellow.
  • Research: strongest premium signal; gold sits next to Amazon's orange/yellow on every card and muddies (research-to-brand #2). Hold gold to the badge and savings line.

C · Lagoon — navy + deep teal

  • One cool accent for price, discount badge and shop label. Nav rule is teal.
  • Keeps continuity with the current site's teal price colour, but re-derived to pass AA.
  • Research: clear of all store-logo colours, passes every criterion; common pairing is the only cost. Recommended (research-to-brand scoring).
